Publishing

There are several ways to publish Inspect Viz content, ranging from PNG images suitable for embedding in documents all the way up to notebooks, websites, and dashboards.

Plots Publishing standalone plots as PNG images or HTML snippets.
Notebooks Publish collections of plots and tables as a notebook.
Websites Embed plots and tables in a larger website.
Dashboards Create a dashboard to provide easy navigation of collections of plots.
PNG Output Rendering plots as PNG images within notebooks, websites, and dashboards.
